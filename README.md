### Featured Projects

#### [Options Pricing & Risk Library](https://github.com/Alexios99/Quantitative-Options-Pricing-Library)
A high-performance library implementing numerical methods for derivatives pricing and risk.
*   **Pricers:** Closed-form (Black-Scholes), lattice (Binomial, Trinomial), Monte Carlo (variance reduction techniques), and PDE Methods (PSOR for American Option).
*   **Model Calibration:** Implemented SABR and Heston models to fit market IV surfaces.

*   **Impact:** **Reduced manual ESG screening time by 90%+**, providing a scalable framework for alternative data integration.
